What is Codencode AI Tracking Agent?

Codencode is a lightweight AI risk intelligence layer that connects to your ERP, project tracking system or Excel workflows. It flags shortages, late deliveries and ownership gaps before they become production or project delays.

Does Codencode replace our ERP?

No. It works above the systems you already use, so teams can gain earlier visibility without a costly ERP replacement or multi-year implementation.

Which risks can it detect?

Projected material shortages, supplier dates that miss milestones, unconfirmed commitments, overdue actions and items with no accountable owner.

Who is it designed for?

Codencode initially serves manufacturers, followed by interior design and small-to-mid construction firms with fragmented purchasing, inventory and project data.

What does A2A mean?

Agent-to-Agent interoperability allows authorised agents across companies, suppliers and systems to exchange risk status and coordinate approved actions with human oversight and audit trails.
